04 / DETAILS

Before you
step inside.

Can I download Ddojcord?

Not yet. This website presents an early concept and visual direction. There is no public installer or released client available.

Does this preview connect to Discord?

No. The interface shown here is a self-contained design study. It does not access accounts, connect to Discord or send messages.

Is Ddojcord an official Discord product?

No. Ddojcord is an independent concept and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Discord Inc.

When will it be available?

There is no release date to announce. The roadmap will change as the concept develops.
